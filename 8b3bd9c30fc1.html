Install
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap Install, then confirm.
  3. Go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
  4. Tap Appy Pie LLC › Trust.
  5. Launch the app from the Home Screen.

Requires iOS 15 or later. The device needs internet access when trusting the certificate.
